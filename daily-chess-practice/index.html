Flexible training, no streak pressure

Build a practical daily chess routine

The Nschess Daily Practice routine suggests three useful activities: learn one idea, solve one Trainer puzzle, and finish a game against a bot. Do them in any order, skip what does not fit, and keep the session manageable.

Open Daily Practice

Three real practice steps

  1. Complete an interactive beginner lesson, including its board task and knowledge check.
  2. Solve a puzzle in the existing Trainer mode.
  3. Finish a game against an AI opponent; a win is not required.

Only completing the underlying activity counts. Opening a page or pressing a button is not completion.

Tracking stays local

Daily Practice uses a UTC calendar date and stores marks in the current browser workspace. Marks are not synced, are not a verified account record, and grant no XP, rating, or reward. Clearing browser data can remove them; today's marks can be reset in the app.

Optional reflection

If a playable saved Review is available on this device, you may reopen it and self-mark that you reflected on a move. This optional step is not part of the three activities and is not presented as a verified completion.
