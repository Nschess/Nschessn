Tactical board practice

Practice chess tactics with clear goals

Choose a puzzle mode, inspect the position, and find the forcing move. Nschess checks moves against chess rules and gives feedback as you work through a line.

Open puzzle practice

Choose a practice mode

  • Trainer: work through a position at your own pace.
  • Rush: solve within a timed session.
  • Streak: keep a run going by finding correct solutions.
  • Solo Battle: use the existing solo puzzle challenge.

These are training modes, not evidence of a rated chess result.

One shared puzzle each UTC day

Open Puzzles and choose Daily · local. Look for a mate in one or a knight fork, ask for a hint when you need a clue, and inspect the solution after solving. Replay lets you revisit the position without changing its saved completion.

Daily attempts, completion, and streaks stay in this browser. Once the app and puzzle assets have been cached, Daily can reopen offline. Clearing browser data can remove your progress. Daily grants no XP, coins, ratings, or synced account rewards.

Build a useful habit

Before moving, ask what the opponent threatens and what your move attacks. Afterward, review why the move works instead of memorizing a result. Short focused sessions can complement a lesson or a game against a computer.
