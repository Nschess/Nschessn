# Nschess asset license and provenance ledger

Status: Release licensing hardening update, checked 2026-09-06.

This ledger records technical provenance and the license information found for assets currently shipped by Nschess. It is not legal clearance. A license marked `A` or `B` still requires the conditions shown in the table to be followed.

## Classification

- `A — VERIFIED SAFE`: a specific permissive/free-software license was located, subject to its notice/source obligations.
- `B — SAFE WITH ATTRIBUTION`: use is permitted when attribution, share-alike, or similar conditions are met.
- `C — COMMERCIAL USE RESTRICTED`: the identified license contains a non-commercial restriction.
- `D — UNCLEAR PROVENANCE`: the source or rights statement is incomplete enough that commercial use cannot be assumed.
- `E — NON-FREE / DO NOT DISTRIBUTE`: the source identifies personal/non-commercial, freeware, or other terms that do not provide a reliable commercial distribution grant.
- `F — HUMAN LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`: the asset may be usable, but the exact license, modification boundary, or provenance needs a human decision.

### Active-theme decision

After Phase C, the selectable/shipped list is limited to the cleared or attributed sets `cburnett`, `chessnut`, `merida`, `fantasy`, `pixel`, `pirouetti`, `kiwen-suwi`, `kosal`, `letter`, and `spatial`. The nine restricted/unresolved directories listed above and `governor` were removed from the release bundle and selector. The former `governor` metadata remains in `assets/app.js` only as a blocked legacy value that resolves to the default set.
