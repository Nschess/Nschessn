Play and practice

Play chess against a computer or challenge a friend

Nschess brings board play, computer opponents, and supported online chess experiences into one browser-based workspace. Start with a bot for a focused practice game, or use online friend and matchmaking features when you are signed in and connected.

Choose a computer opponent

Practice against a bot

Select an opponent and play a legal chess game on the interactive board. Bot presets are practice choices, not independently calibrated Elo ratings. Optional engine assets may need to load before play.

If the engine is unavailable, Nschess reports that limitation rather than promising a strength it cannot provide.

Play online when connected

Friend challenges and matchmaking require an internet connection and the relevant account/session state. Server-backed game and result data remain authoritative; a local practice game is not an online match record.

Learn between games

Use a beginner lesson to learn a rule, try a tactical puzzle, and review a completed game to reflect on a decision. Practice marks and online results are kept distinct.
