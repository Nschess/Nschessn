Guided beginner foundations

Learn chess fundamentals one move at a time

Nschess Learn offers 68 guided board exercises for building confidence with the rules and patterns that appear in real games. Read a short explanation, make the legal move on the board, and answer a quick knowledge check.

What you can practice

  • Board setup, piece movement, captures, and king safety.
  • Check, checkmate, castling, en passant, and promotion.
  • Opening habits and tactical ideas such as forks, pins, and discovered attacks.

The current catalog is focused on beginner foundations; it does not claim a complete advanced curriculum.

Learn by making a move

Each supported lesson pairs an explanation with an interactive board task and a short check-in. Hints and replay help you retry a line. Lesson checklist state is stored locally in this browser and is not a server-verified account completion or reward.

Keep learning

Use a lesson idea in a Trainer puzzle, then try it in a game against a computer opponent. Nschess does not promise rating gains; improvement depends on practice.
